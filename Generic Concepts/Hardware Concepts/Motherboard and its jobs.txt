A motherboard is something to which everything is connected to, whether it is a CPU, a GPU, a USB flash drive, a SSD, or a NIC (Network Interface Card),
because at the end of the day you need some way to make devices talk to each other, even if you made everything wireless, then too the thing processing
the radio signals will be connected to a motherboard. 

In other words, a motherboard is a must. It provides sets of wires (usually copper) which connects everything, it also provides
slots to insert devices, like a CPU slot and pins for a particular brand of CPU, a PCIe slot for graphic cards and NVMe SSDs and so on.

A thing to note, anything other than the CPU is made to fit inside a motherboard, but motherboard manufactures make motherboards according to 
the CPU it is going to host.
